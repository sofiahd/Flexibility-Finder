Concept for RED™

The Flexibility
Finder

RED™ already gives businesses lower bills and transparent pricing. This tool takes the next step — helping any RED™ customer, from a small café to a large logistics site, discover which of their appliances and systems have flexible load, see what that's worth in £, and find out the best time of day to run each one.

2-minute setup Works for any business size Saving on top of RED™ base saving

Why this exists: RED™ customers of every size have appliances and systems where when they run matters as much as how much they use. A café with a dishwasher, a gym with showers, a warehouse with an EV van— all businesses have flexibility they haven't seen the value of yet. This tool finds it and puts a number on it.
